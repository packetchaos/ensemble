The Problem

CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog is the clearest "fix this now" signal in security — and each entry carries a federal remediation due date. Pulling KEV out of thousands of findings, matching it to due dates, and handing it to a patch team is tedious and easy to let slide.

What It Does

Laelaps finds every asset with a KEV finding from the CISA KEV reference Tenable attaches to each finding, joins it to CISA's catalog for due dates and ransomware use, and shows what is overdue, what is due soon, which crown jewels and vendors are involved — then tags the assets so the work can be routed.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • KEV from your own findings — Tenable marks each KEV finding with a CISA cross-reference and the date CISA added it; Laelaps reads that, so no memorized CVE list is involved.
  • Due dates and overdue — CISA's remediation due date per KEV, on a month-by-month timeline of overdue vs due later.
  • Ransomware, crown jewels, vendors — KEVs CISA links to ransomware campaigns, crown jewels (ACR above 7) carrying KEV, and the vendors and products involved.
  • Four tag schemes — CISA KEV : Vulnerable, CISA KEV : <date added>, CISA KEV Due : <due date> and CISA KEV : Ransomware — each on the exact assets.
  • Hunt in plain English — ask a KEV question and get the answer from one read-only SQL query over navi.db and its tags.
  • Approved downloads — the CISA catalog is fetched only after you approve it (or auto-approve in Settings), with a bundled snapshot when offline.
  • By tag — run over one tag's assets: KEV exposure, CISA due-date compliance, crown jewels, vendors and dates added are rebuilt for those assets, with the same per-CVE due dates; tags written from that view land only on them. Switch between the whole estate and your last 5 tags without re-running.

How It Works

Laelaps reads the cross-references on findings in your local navi.db and joins CISA's KEV catalog by CVE. Vulnerable and date-added tags use navi's own --xrefs CISA-KNOWN-EXPLOITED / --xid selectors; Due and Ransomware tags use a tag-by-query on the assets with an open finding for those CVEs. All behind the write gate and a confirm.

Who It's For

Vulnerability management leads who need a defensible "patch this first" list, and teams accountable for CISA due dates.
