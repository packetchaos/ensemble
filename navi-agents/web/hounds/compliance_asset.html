COMPLIANCE DRILL-DOWN
Compliance
“See why an asset fails audit — and the vulnerabilities behind it.”

The Problem

A failed audit check says what is wrong but not how much it matters. Teams fix controls in isolation, without seeing that the same weakness also shows up as open vulnerabilities on the same host — or which benchmark is dragging the estate down.

What It Does

Compliance Drill-down investigates compliance two ways. By asset: every audit check the asset passed, failed or warned, with actual vs expected values and framework mappings, plus the inferred link between each failed control and real vulnerabilities on that host. By audit file: a benchmark leaderboard, each file's adherence, the checks and assets it covers, and the vulnerabilities behind its failing controls.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Every check on one asset — status, actual vs expected value, and the frameworks and controls it maps to.
  • Audit ↔ vuln correlation — compliance results carry no CVEs, so Compliance Drill-down infers the link by topic on the same asset — and shows the evidence.
  • Audit-file lens — a leaderboard of benchmarks by adherence, then one file's checks, assets and the vulnerabilities behind its failures.
  • Estate-wide links — the audit-to-vulnerability correlations across every asset, not just one.
  • Opened from Compliance Overview — a Compliance Overview card (failed, a control, a framework, an audit file, under 50% pass) narrows the asset list and opens its worst asset.
  • Audit-Vuln Link tags — tag the asset with a correlated topic you choose, so the joint fix can be routed.

How It Works

Compliance Drill-down reads the compliance and vulns tables in your local navi.db. The correlation is inferred by keyword topic, never claimed as a CVE match. The Audit-Vuln Link tag goes through navi's tag commands behind the write gate and a confirm.

Who It's For

Auditors and GRC analysts preparing evidence, and system owners who need to know why their host fails and what to fix first.

Works Better With

Compliance Overview gives the estate-wide verdict and hands each group to Compliance Drill-down. Asset Dossier and Web App Dossier are the matching deep-dives for an asset's findings and for a web app.
