CRITICALITY CALIBRATION
Ownership & SLA
“Make risk scores reflect your business reality.”

The Problem

Out-of-the-box Asset Criticality Ratings rarely match reality: the test box scores like a crown jewel while the payment server sits at a default. Tenable One's exposure scores, and every specialist that ranks by ACR, inherit that one wrong number.

What It Does

Criticality Calibration changes the ACR of every asset that carries a tag — set it to a value, or raise or lower it by N — with a business reason Tenable records alongside it. It shows exactly what navi will change before anything is written, logs every change, and can protect the assets it changed from later overwrites.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Set, +N or −N per tag — pick any tag from the live Tenable tag list and calibrate every asset on it at once; ACR stays within 1–10.
  • Business case on every change — Business Critical, In Scope For Compliance, Existing Mitigation Control or Dev Only, plus an optional note — sent to Tenable with the change.
  • Preview before you write — per change: how many assets will change, how many are protected, how many have no ACR yet and will be skipped.
  • Bulk in plain English — describe the calibration ("production to 9, lab down 2") and an optional AI model drafts the changes; only existing tags and scores 1–10 pass, and you approve each.
  • Protection with NO : UPDATE — tag the assets the Ensemble changed so later bulk changes leave them alone — navi skips any asset tagged exactly NO : UPDATE.
  • ACR log + CSV — every change the Ensemble made: asset, before and after, selector, business case — filterable and exportable.

How It Works

The tag list comes live from navi explore info tags; preview and protection read your local navi.db. Changes run through navi enrich acr (set / inc / dec), behind the write gate and an explicit confirm. A new NO : UPDATE tag counts for navi after the next asset sync.

Who It's For

Vulnerability management leads whose priorities depend on criticality, and risk owners who know which assets actually matter.

Works Better With

Tag first, then calibrate: Linnaeus, Ownership and Agent Group Sync lay down the business tags Criticality Calibration calibrates against, and Automation Station keeps them current and can apply ACR rules on its schedule. Fenrir (crown jewels = ACR ≥ 7) and Exposure Score (crown jewels = ACR ≥ 9) both rank by the result.

Why It's Different

  • No surprises — the preview mirrors navi's own selection logic, so the count you approve is the count that changes.
  • Justified, not arbitrary — every change carries Tenable's reason flags and is kept in the ACR log for audit.
  • Protects its own work — NO : UPDATE stops a later bulk change from undoing a deliberate calibration; only Tag Removal removes it.
