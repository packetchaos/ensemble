“Reveal the AI hiding in your estate.”
Pythia, the Oracle of Delphi — here she finds the oracles in your estate: the AI / ML software, model hosts and the exposure around them.

The Problem

AI and ML tools spread faster than any inventory can track — Ollama, Jupyter, vector databases, AI coding tools — and many can send data to third-party APIs. A plugin-only view misses most of them, so shadow AI piles up unseen until an auditor or an incident finds it.

What It Does

Pythia finds AI across five data sources, gives each asset a role, flags endpoints that are exposed or unauthenticated, maps where data can leave (sanctioned vs shadow), checks KEV, critical and end-of-life risk, maps MITRE ATLAS techniques, and compares each asset's ACR with the ACR its role suggests.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Five-source discovery — Tenable's Artificial Intelligence plugin family, the software and CPE inventories, plugin output, and network endpoints — one AI asset list.
  • Role and suggested ACR — Model Serving, Vector DB / RAG, GPU / Training, MLOps, Notebook / Dev, AI Dev Tool, LLM API Client — each with a suggested ACR (e.g. Model Serving 9).
  • Exposed and unauthenticated endpoints — AI services listening on inference, notebook or MLOps ports, with an unauthenticated check from the plugin output.
  • Data-egress governance — egress capability and destinations seen in plugin output, split sanctioned vs shadow by an allowlist you edit.
  • Why + false-positive control — every row shows its evidence (source, term, plugin, snippet); dismiss a false positive per asset, per framework or everywhere.
  • MITRE ATLAS map — AI-specific adversary techniques implied by each asset's role, exposure and egress — click a technique or tactic to list and tag its assets.
  • Gated tags and ACR — AI, AI Role, AI Software, AI Exposed, AI Priority, AI Egress and AI ATLAS tags; under-rated assets get AI ACR Raise and the role's suggested ACR.
  • By tag — run over one tag's assets: the same per-asset roles, exposure, risk and ACR as the whole estate, with every count, percentage and tag write limited to that tag. Switch between the whole estate and your last 5 tags without re-running.

How It Works

Pythia reads your local navi.db (vulns, plugin output, software, cpes); with Optimize the egress search runs inside the database. The ATLAS mapping is built in. Tags go through navi's tag-by-query and ACR through navi enrich acr — each behind the write gate and a confirm.

Who It's For

AI governance and risk teams who need a defensible inventory, security architects deciding what to sanction, and CISOs asked "how much AI do we run, and where does its data go?"
