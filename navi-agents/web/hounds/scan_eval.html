SCAN HEALTH
Coverage
“Know what your scans can't see — before you trust them.”

The Problem

Every dashboard and priority list is only as good as the scans underneath it. An uncredentialed host reports a fraction of its real findings, a failed login looks like a clean host, and an old scan quietly ages the data — and nobody notices until a "clean" asset turns out to have been half-blind.

What It Does

Scan Health reads Tenable's scan-information and credential-status plugins to show which assets were scanned with a working login, which were blind and why, how fresh each scan is, which crown jewels sit in a blind spot, and how scanners, policies and scans perform. Every set can be tagged so the fix gets routed.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • One credentialed rule — credentialed = the scan-information plugin reports credentialed checks, or a valid-credentials plugin is present; blind = scanned without credentials. Every specialist in the Ensemble uses this rule.
  • Why a host is blind — credential failed, insufficient privilege, intermittent, or no credentials at all — and credentialed hosts that still have a credential issue.
  • Freshness — scanned in the last 7 days, 8–30, 31–90, over 90, never, or date unknown; network vs agent scans.
  • Crown-jewel blind spots — crown jewels (ACR at or above the line in Settings, 8 by default) that are uncredentialed or failed, or not scanned in over 90 days.
  • Performance and scanners — average scan time, slow scans, slow scanners, policies and scans; scanner load; scanners on an old plugin feed; safe-checks scans.
  • Scan Health tags — Cred Failure, Insufficient Privilege, Intermittent Auth, Uncredentialed, Patch Assessment Failed, Stale Scan 30d+ / 90d+, Never Scanned, Slow Scan, Crown Jewel Blind Spot, and by scanner, policy or scan.
  • By tag — run over one tag's assets: credential health, freshness, blind spots and scanner / policy load are rebuilt for just those assets, and Scan Health tags written from that view stay inside the tag. Switch between the whole estate and your last 5 tags without re-running.

How It Works

Scan Health reproduces navi scan evaluate over your local navi.db — no extra scanning. The rule exactly: plugin 19506 says "Credentialed checks : yes", or plugin 141118 or 110095 is present. The asset list pages and filters on the server and exports to CSV. Tagging re-runs the evaluation on the server and tags the exact assets under Scan Health, behind the write gate and a confirm.

Who It's For

Scan engineers tuning credentials, scanners and policies, and VM leads who want to know how much of the estate their numbers actually describe.

Works Better With

Scan Health's rule decides "blind" for the whole pack — Exposure Score's confidence, Identity Inventory's coverage, Fenrir's uncredentialed hosts, Alerts's coverage alert and Ensemble Report's funnel. Remote Remediation's credential test tells you why a login fails, and Automation Station re-applies the Scan Health tags on its schedule.
