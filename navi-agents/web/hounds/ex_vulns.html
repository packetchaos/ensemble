Platform
“Hunt any finding — by plugin, CVE, severity or finding ID.”

The Problem

Finding every instance of one vulnerability — a CVE in the news, a plugin a vendor just flagged — shouldn't mean writing SQL or waiting on a report.

What It Does

Explorer › Vulnerabilities searches every finding in navi.db, one row each, by plugin name or ID, CVE, severity or finding ID, and shows each finding with its asset and VPR. A finding ID opens it in Tenable, the asset opens Asset Dossier, and a fix can be handed to Remote Remediation.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Search by anything — plugin name, plugin ID, CVE, severity or finding ID.
  • Findings in context — asset, severity and VPR on every row.
  • Open in Tenable — a finding ID opens that finding in Tenable.
  • Deep-dive and fix — the asset opens Asset Dossier; Remediate hands the plugin and host to Remote Remediation, pre-filled, never auto-run.
  • Email the result — send the rows to an owner through Notify & Report.

How It Works

Explorer › Vulnerabilities runs a read-only search over the findings in your local navi.db; results page on the server, 20 rows a page (up to 50).

Who It's For

Analysts answering "where do we have this?" and responders scoping a new CVE.

Works Better With

Part of the search pack with Explorer › Assets, Explorer › Plugins, Explorer › Routes and Explorer › Paths. Laelaps and Exposure Score rank what Explorer › Vulnerabilities finds; Remote Remediation fixes it.
