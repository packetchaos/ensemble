COMPLIANCE OVERVIEW
Compliance
“Weigh the estate against its benchmarks — and assign failures an owner.”

The Problem

Tenable's compliance scans produce one result per check per asset — tens of thousands of rows that say little on their own. Which controls fail most widely, which frameworks are exposed, and who should fix them gets lost in the volume.

What It Does

Compliance Overview turns your audit results into one verdict: pass rate, results by status, the controls failing the most assets, how failures map to frameworks such as NIST 800-53, PCI, HIPAA, CIS and ISO, every audit benchmark, and a scorecard per asset. He can tag an Audit Owner on the assets failing a control.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Results by status — passed, failed, warning and unknown across every check × asset, with pass rate = pass ÷ (pass + fail).
  • Controls failing the most assets — the short list of controls whose fix moves the most assets.
  • Framework exposure — failures rolled up by the frameworks and controls each check references (NIST, PCI, HIPAA, CIS, ISO …).
  • Audit benchmarks — every audit file in navi.db, with its results.
  • Per-asset scorecards — which assets pass least, which have warnings, which are clean.
  • Audit Owner tags — assign an owner to the assets that fail one control — Audit Owner : <owner>.
  • One click to the evidence — every card, bar, control, framework and benchmark opens the matching assets in Compliance Drill-down.
  • By tag — run over one tag's assets: pass rate, failing controls, frameworks, benchmarks and scorecards are rebuilt over that tag, and Audit Owner tagging from that view reaches only the tag's failing assets. Switch between the whole estate and your last 5 tags without re-running.

How It Works

Compliance Overview reads the compliance table in your local navi.db (it parses the framework references navi stores for each check). It changes nothing except the Audit Owner tag, which goes through navi's tag commands behind the write gate and a confirm.

Who It's For

GRC and audit teams, compliance owners preparing for an assessment, and security leads who need to show how the estate measures against a framework.

Works Better With

Compliance Drill-down is his twin for one asset at a time. Fenrir turns failed controls such as LAPS and SMB signing into attack-path steps, and Exposure Score counts compliance failures in its score. Automation Station can keep Audit Owner tags current once an owner is set.
