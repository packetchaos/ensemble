ALERTS
Platform
“Get one alert when a condition changes — not one every cycle.”

The Problem

The conditions that matter — credentialed coverage slipping, a new CISA KEV on your assets, certificates about to expire — change between the times anyone looks. Dashboards wait to be opened, and alerts that fire every cycle get muted.

What It Does

Alerts watches navi.db for the conditions you choose and emails a digest when one newly fires. An alert is a number plus a condition: above or below a threshold, or a rise or drop since the last check. Start from a suggested pattern, or describe the alert in plain English, then test it before you save it.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Seven ready-made patterns — credentialed-scan coverage, slow scans, new CISA KEV exposure, new criticals, certificates expiring within 30 days, ownerless assets, end-of-life software.
  • Alerts in plain English — describe what to watch and Alerts drafts a read-only SQL metric, operator and threshold for review; without an AI model it builds the query from navi's tables.
  • Test before you save — a dry run shows the current value and whether it would fire — nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
  • Fires on change — an alert notifies when it goes from clear to firing, once, until it clears again — a lasting condition doesn't email every cycle.
  • One digest per check — everything that newly fired in one email, with the affected hosts, to an allowed recipient.
  • History — recent evaluations: what fired, what cleared, and the value each time.

How It Works

Every metric is a read-only query over your local navi.db, or a built-in check (credential coverage uses the Ensemble's shared credentialed rule). Alerts are saved locally. They are evaluated on demand or in the Contract's loop; the digest is sent with navi mail only when email is enabled (NAVI_EMAIL) and the recipient is allowed.

Who It's For

Vulnerability management leads and SOC teams who want to hear about a change without watching a dashboard all day.

Works Better With

Alerts watches the numbers the other specialists produce — Scan Health's coverage, Laelaps's KEV, Certania's certificates, Exposure Routes' ownership, Charon's EOL.
