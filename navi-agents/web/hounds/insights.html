The Problem

Each specialist answers its own question well, but a CISO doesn't want twenty answers — they want one picture and one next move. Without a view that brings them together, the most dangerous risk survives: the unknown unknowns nobody thought to ask a single tool about.

What It Does

Ensemble Report reads what the whole pack found on its last release and puts it on one page, Ensemble Report: how exposed the estate is, where risk concentrates, what is converging, what is on the clock, the blind spots, and the next moves — including a recommended play and how much exposure it would remove. Every number opens the exact assets behind it.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • At a glance, actionable — every card opens its evidence: the exact assets (KEV, score ≥ 60, crown jewels, with KEV or blind, EOL, quantum-vulnerable …) or the owning specialist, already filtered.
  • Coverage funnel — all assets → seen by a specialist → credentialed → classified → owned → business-tiered, each step linked to the specialist that owns the gap.
  • Dark matter — assets no specialist saw, blind hosts, unclassified, unowned, no business tier, shadow AI.
  • Who's barking — the worst assets by fused score, with each specialist's points on each host; the estate as one square per asset.
  • Converging signals — signal stacks, the overlap map and cross-specialist surprises — where several specialists fire on the same assets.
  • Recommended play — patch KEV / critical, replace EOL, renew certificates, segment exposure or assign owners — with the exposure it would remove and the signals it neutralizes.
  • What changed — the digest of what moved since the last release.

How It Works

Ensemble Report reads the cached results of the last Run the Ensemble plus Exposure Score's fused scores; it writes nothing. Evidence lists are computed on demand by the specialist that owns each number, so they match the card.

Who It's For

CISOs who want the estate on one page, VM leads running the morning stand-up, and anyone building executive reporting that survives questions.
