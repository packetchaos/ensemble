“See every expiring certificate before it breaks something.”
Certania, from “certificate” — a watcher over the chain of trust, finding the certificates that are expired, failing or about to expire.

The Problem

Certificate expiries cause outages and silent trust failures, and most teams find out when something breaks. The renewal that slipped takes a customer-facing service down at 2 a.m., and the post-mortem says "we didn't know it was expiring."

What It Does

Certania finds every certificate that has already expired — month by month over the last 12 months, plus everything older that is still deployed — and every certificate expiring in the next 12 months, maps each to its assets, and lays them out on one timeline. Certania tags the assets with their exact expiry date so the renewal becomes routable work.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Expired and expiring inventory — expired by month for the last 12 months and older, plus everything failing in the next 12 months, each with its assets.
  • One expiry timeline — older, the last 12 months and the next 12 months on one axis, so renewals are planned by month, not found by outage.
  • Risk context — crown jewels (ACR above 7) with an expired or expiring certificate, weak crypto (MD5 / SHA-1 signatures, RSA keys under 2048 bits), self-signed certificates, and days expired.
  • Cert failure : <date> tags — the assets behind each expiry date you choose, tagged with that exact date — a dated, routable work item.
  • Cert Issue tags by plugin — every certificate plugin firing in navi.db, with its asset count; tag its assets Cert Issue : <plugin name>.
  • Devices and apps from certificates — device and application names identified from certificate subjects.
  • By tag — run over one tag's assets: expired and failing certificates, crown jewels, cert-issue plugins and IoT / app fingerprints for just those assets, with the same per-certificate verdicts; tags written from that view touch only them. Switch between the whole estate and your last 5 tags without re-running.

How It Works

Certania reads the certs table in your local navi.db (it understands every date format navi stores) and certificate plugins in vulns. It is deterministic — no AI reasoning. Lists page and search on the server; tags are written through navi's tag commands behind the write gate and a confirm.

Who It's For

PKI owners who need a renewal calendar, ops and SRE teams tired of expiry-driven incidents, and vulnerability management teams closing the trust gap.

Works Better With

Heimdall reads the same certificates for quantum-vulnerable crypto, Exposure Score counts weak or expiring crypto in its score, Alerts can warn when certificates expire within 30 days, and Ensemble Report's expired card opens Certania already filtered. Automation Station re-applies the Cert failure tags on its schedule.

Why It's Different

  • A calendar, not a list — expired and expiring certificates by month, readable in seconds.
  • Dated, gated tags — Cert failure : <date> turns an expiry into an assigned task, approved before it's written.
  • No guessing — deterministic rules from navi.db, so the same data always gives the same answer.
