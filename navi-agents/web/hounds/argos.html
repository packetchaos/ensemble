ASSET DOSSIER
Asset Truth
“Pull one asset's whole story into a single dossier.”

The Problem

When one asset matters — an incident, an audit, a crown jewel — its story is scattered across findings, tags, software, certificates, scan data and ownership. Pulling it together by hand takes the time you don't have when it counts.

What It Does

Give Asset Dossier a UUID or an IP and he assembles one dossier: identity and host profile, the asset's Exposure Score and rank, open findings and CVEs, SLA clock, compliance, certificates, EOL software, owners, identities, scan confidence, and every tag the Ensemble put on it — each explained, with the specialist that raised it.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Identity and host profile — hostname, IP, OS, network, ACR, AES, CPU / RAM / architecture from inventory plugins, last scan and its duration, and a Tenable link.
  • Exposure Score in context — this asset's Exposure Score score and band, and its rank in the estate.
  • Every tag deciphered — what each tag means and which specialist raised it — Laelaps for KEV, Certania for certificates, Charon for EOL, and the rest.
  • Findings with a fix path — top findings and CVEs with plugin output on demand; each one can be sent to Remote Remediation for remediation.
  • Owners and identities — who owns the asset, with a link to assign one, and the accounts seen on the host.
  • Software, certificates, web pages — installed software, certificates with expiry and weak crypto, and web pages captured by plugins.
  • Live views from navi — per-asset software, patches and details pulled live from the navi CLI on demand.

How It Works

Asset Dossier reads your local navi.db and changes nothing; the live views run navi explore uuid on demand. Every specialist's asset rows and the evidence lists link here.

Who It's For

Incident responders who need one asset's full story fast, analysts triaging a host, and anyone reviewing a crown jewel before a decision.

Works Better With

Asset Dossier is where the Ensemble's per-asset work meets: Exposure Score's score, SLA Tracker's SLA clock, Compliance Overview and Compliance Drill-down for compliance, Certania, Charon, Scan Health's scan confidence and Exposure Routes' owners. From a finding, Remote Remediation can push the fix.
