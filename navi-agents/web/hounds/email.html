NOTIFY & REPORT
Ownership & SLA
“Deliver every finding to the person who owns it.”

The Problem

Findings that never reach the right person never get fixed. A report sent to everyone is read by no one, and an owner who has to dig through a portal for their own assets usually doesn't.

What It Does

Notify & Report turns the Ensemble's findings into email routed by owner: each owner gets only their assets, with links back into Tenable. He also sends CISA KEV fire alarms, certificate countdowns, the Ensemble Report morning briefing, Contract plans and results — and any chart, list or dashboard in the app. You always preview first; sending is gated.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Owner-routed remediation — reads Owner tags from Exposure Routes and emails each owner only their assets, each with its Tenable asset link and the plugin links for its findings.
  • Seven report types — owner remediation, vulnerability detail, KEV fire alarm, certificate countdown, morning briefing, Contract plan and Contract result.
  • Board or technical — summary counts for leadership, or per-asset, per-finding detail for the people who fix it.
  • Email anything on screen — any widget's picture and its list (a CSV attachment at 100 rows or more), a Sankey graph, or a whole Custom Dashboard.
  • From any specialist — the Email findings button carries a specialist's assets, finding IDs and the tag it applied straight into a draft.
  • Sends exactly what you previewed — the exact previewed messages are sent, and every send is recorded in the Tagging log.

How It Works

Notify & Report composes from your local navi.db without changing it, and sends with navi action mail — SMTP is configured once, outside the app, with navi config smtp. A send needs writes enabled, email enabled (NAVI_EMAIL) and an explicit confirm.

Who It's For

Vulnerability management teams who need findings to reach asset owners, team leads who want each owner to see only their work, and CISOs who want the briefing in their inbox.

Works Better With

Exposure Routes' Owner tags decide who gets what; Laelaps supplies the KEV alarm, Certania the certificate countdown, Ensemble Report the morning briefing, and Automation Station the per-cycle summary. Alerts sends its digest through the same mail path.
