EXPOSURE ROUTES
Ownership & SLA
“Put an owner on every exposure route — and show the gaps.”

The Problem

Thousands of findings only become a manageable list when someone owns them. navi already groups findings into application routes and filesystem paths — but nobody has said who owns each one, so the unowned ones rot until they surface as an incident.

What It Does

Exposure Routes maps every application route and filesystem path to an owner, in plain English — "give the payment routes to VM Team, put /opt under Networking, ignore the ICMP route" — and tags every asset on a matched route or path Owner : <route or path>: <owner>. He measures coverage honestly: an asset is closed only when every route it sits on has an owner.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Owners in plain English — describe the mapping; Exposure Routes resolves each owner against your Tenable users and groups and previews it before tagging.
  • Owners from Tenable — users, user groups and agent groups pulled live through navi (the Tenable MCP as a backup), cached so the page stays fast.
  • Tags on the right assets — Owner tags on every asset on a matched route or with a matched path, never removing anything.
  • Decisions, not just owners — mark a route ignored, accepted or mitigated, and the coverage counts it as handled.
  • Coverage you can defend — routes owned, partial or unowned; paths owned; assets fully closed; ownerless risk by VPR.
  • Ownership Sankey and map — routes and paths flowing to their owners, the gaps, and each owner's load; drill into any new path.
  • By tag — run over one tag's assets: ownership coverage, the Sankey and the Ownership Map are rebuilt over the routes and paths those assets sit on, and owner tags written from that view land only on that tag's assets. Switch between the whole estate and your last 5 tags without re-running.

How It Works

Exposure Routes reads routes and paths from your local navi.db and owners live through navi. Plain-English owner assignment uses the configured AI model; the Tag owner panel and ignore / accept / mitigate instructions work without one. Tags go through navi's tag commands behind the write gate and a confirm; Refresh runs a background navi update assets so new Owner tags count.

Who It's For

VM leads who need every finding to have an owner, team leads who want only their slice, and CISOs who must show accountability coverage.

Works Better With

Ownership rolls Exposure Routes' owners up to each asset, Notify & Report emails each owner only their assets, SLA Tracker measures SLA by owner, and Explorer › Routes, Explorer › Paths and Custom App Discovery work the same routes and paths. Automation Station can keep the Owner tags current from a mapping in its policy.
