Ownership

Ownership from the asset's point of view: who owns each asset, how much of it is claimed, what isn't owned, and what you're blind to — plus your crown jewels (ACR ≥ 9) and what the Ensemble already know about them. Three layers: Business Owner (leaders own groups/apps), Owner (Exposure Routes — routes & paths), and a single Asset Owner who owns every open finding on an asset (or just the rest) — routes and paths are how findings get assigned.

Press ▶ Run

Rolls up ownership for every asset in navi.db — the three owner layers, crown jewels, blind spots and who owns what. Your last run is kept on the server, so coming back restores it instantly (and Run the Ensemble can run it for you).

📊 At a glance

Every number opens what it counts — a filter on the per-asset table below (assign an owner right there), a section of this page, or the specialist that owns the data (Exposure Routes, Asset Dossier, Exposure Score, SLA Tracker).

✨ Ask — tag owners in plain English

Describe who owns which assets. The processor reads each asset's tags and attributes (e.g. Business Tier, Environment, ACR, crown-jewel). A group assignment writes a Business Owner; “complete / single / every route & path / the rest” writes a single Asset Owner that closes the asset.

👑 Crown jewels — what the Ensemble know

Your most business-critical assets (ACR ≥ 9) — is each one owned, and what have the other specialists already found on it?

🕳 Unclaimed & blind spots — what am I blind to?

Assets on the attack surface with no owner — crown jewels and high-ACR first. 🏷️ Tag owner inline, or tag one owner on all shown at once. Tagging writes an Asset Owner tag and cascades Owner tags across the asset's routes & paths (gated).
